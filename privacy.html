Privacy Policy

GeorgeTech PC Control
Last updated: 24 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GeorgeTech PC Control (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal data when you use the GeorgeTech PC Control website, mobile app, Alexa skill, Windows agent, local wake gateway, and related services.

1. Who we are

GeorgeTech PC Control is a remote PC-control service designed to allow authorised users to wake, shut down, restart, and monitor eligible computers and supporting gateway devices.

Contact email: support@georgetech.uk

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address and login details.
  • Device information, such as device names, MAC addresses, local network wake settings, gateway identifiers, and PC agent identifiers.
  • Command and status data, such as wake, shutdown, restart, status checks, last seen times, and command history.
  • Authentication and linking data, such as account-linking tokens and session information required for Alexa and other supported integrations.
  • Technical and diagnostic information, such as logs, errors, app version, browser details, and IP address information used for security and troubleshooting.

3. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • link and manage authorised PCs, gateways, and integrations;
  • send and process remote control commands;
  • display device state and service status;
  • protect the service from misuse, fraud, or unauthorised access;
  • diagnose errors, improve reliability, and provide support.

4. Legal basis

Where UK GDPR applies, our main lawful bases are:

  • Contract – to provide the service you have requested.
  • Legitimate interests – for security, fraud prevention, diagnostics, service improvement, and support.
  • Consent – where specifically required, for example for optional communications or certain integrations.

5. Account linking and Alexa

If you link GeorgeTech PC Control with Amazon Alexa, we process the information required to authenticate you, authorise the link, and allow the skill to discover and control devices associated with your account. Alexa voice interactions are also subject to Amazon’s own privacy terms.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with service providers only where necessary to operate the service, such as hosting, authentication, database, or infrastructure providers. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our legal rights.

7. Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for account management, service delivery, security, diagnostics, legal compliance, and dispute resolution. Different categories of logs and device records may be retained for different periods depending on their purpose.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including authentication controls, encrypted connections, role-based access, and logging. No system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so you should also keep your own devices and passwords secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to certain processing of your personal data, and to request a copy of your information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above.

10. Third-party services

GeorgeTech PC Control may rely on trusted third-party infrastructure and integrations, including account-linking and cloud infrastructure services. Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices where applicable.

11. Children

This service is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for adults and authorised users only.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: support@georgetech.uk
